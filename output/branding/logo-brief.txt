Metria — comparison aperture

Original symbol for reference/candidate inference verification. Two related,
interlocking forms frame a shared center. No letters or wordmark. Transparent
background. Cobalt #406CE3 and periwinkle #A8BDFF.

Creative exploration used the built-in image-generation tool. The production
SVG was drawn directly as geometric paths for clean edges and scalability.

Generation prompt:
Create one original symbol-only logo for Metria, an open-source tool for verifying
LLM inference changes by comparing reference and candidate runs. A compact
geometric comparison aperture: two clean, balanced, interlocking folded forms
facing a shared central diamond of negative space. Strong silhouette, optical
balance, consistent visual weight, recognizable at favicon size. Cobalt blue
#406CE3 and periwinkle #A8BDFF. Crisp vector-like edges and softly rounded exterior
corners. Transparent canvas and negative space. No letters, wordmark, text,
rounded-square app tile, badge, infinity sign, arrows, gradient, shadow, bevel,
3D render, mockup scene, or watermark.
